Along with milk, we sell fresh chakki atta (wheat flour) at Rs 170 per kg. It is ground on our own chakki.

How we make it

  1. Wash the wheat.
  2. Clean it to remove stones, dust and husk.
  3. Grind it on our own chakki.

Nothing is added and nothing is taken out, so you get whole-wheat atta with its natural bran.

Chakki atta vs packet flour

Most packet flour is ground in big roller mills, where part of the bran is removed for a whiter colour. Chakki atta is ground whole, so it is slightly darker and coarser.
