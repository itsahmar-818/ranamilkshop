Privacy Policy

Rana Ahmad Milk Shop, Ghalla Mandi (near Central Jail), Multan, Pakistan. Last updated: 6 October 2026.

This policy explains what information Rana Ahmad Milk Shop ("we") collects when you contact us, including through our WhatsApp ordering assistant, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name when you message us.
  • The messages you send us, such as product questions, order details (items and quantity) and your delivery address.
  • Bill and payment records needed to run your account with the shop.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions about products, rates, timings and delivery.
  • To take, deliver and confirm your orders.
  • To prepare and share your bills.

Our WhatsApp assistant uses an automated AI service to read your message and write a reply. Your message text is sent to that service only to create the reply.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. It is only shared with the services needed to run the assistant and deliver messages (such as Meta/WhatsApp and our AI provider), or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order and bill records for as long as needed to serve you and keep proper accounts. Chat context used by the assistant is kept only for a short time.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your information at any time. Send a WhatsApp message saying "Delete my data" to 0323 6717478. We will delete your chat and contact details within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.
