Cow, buffalo or mix: which one?

Buffalo milk is thicker and creamier. Many families prefer it for tea, khoya and making dahi at home. Cow milk is lighter and easier for some people to digest. A mix gives you a bit of both. Tell us what you like and we will send the same milk every day.

Why cheap milk is cheap

When milk is sold far below the market rate, something has usually been added to it: water, milk powder, or chemicals that make it last longer. Pure milk from healthy animals costs more to produce. We would rather explain our price than water down our milk.

Where our milk comes from

We keep our own herd of Murrah buffaloes and Sahiwal cows. The milk comes straight from our farm to the shop in Ghalla Mandi, near Central Jail, and from there to your home.
