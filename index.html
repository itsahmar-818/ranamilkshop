Questions, answered.

Everything people usually ask before their first order.

Do you deliver milk at home in Multan?

Yes. We deliver fresh milk, dahi, malai, desi ghee and aata at home across Multan, morning and evening. Delivery charges depend on your route.

What are your shop timings?

We are open every day from 6:00 am to 11:00 am and from 6:00 pm to 11:00 pm.

Which milk do you sell?

Pure cow milk, buffalo milk, or a mix of both. No water, no powder and no preservatives.

How should I store the milk?

Boil the milk within 2 hours of purchase. Fresh milk without preservatives spoils quickly, especially in summer.

How can I pay?

Cash, EasyPaisa, JazzCash or bank transfer.

Do you give a discount on bulk orders?

Yes, bulk orders can get a discount. Message us on WhatsApp at 0323 6717478.
